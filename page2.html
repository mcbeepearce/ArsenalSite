Arsenal in EAFC 27

EAFC is the continuation of the iconic "FIFA" video game series, with EAFC 27 being the most recent release, coming out in less than a week. With the game having such a large and diverse database, it is no surprise that Arsenal is in the game and is also one of the best teams in the game. You can play as Arsenal in many different modes; however, the Manager Career Mode gives you the most hands-on experience, as you are given the freedom to sign and sell players, customize the team's tactics, and play all of the games.

Arsenal in Manager Career Mode

The Arsenal squad is very balanced, with 13 players rated 84 or higher. However, there are still a handful of question marks surrounding certain players, the tactics of the team, and players they might sign. When selecting Arsenal in this mode, you are given the ability to control all of these things and more. You even have the ability to lead Arsenal FC and the fans to their first UEFA Champions League trophy, and continue to compete within England by winning the Premier League, FA Cup, Carabao Cup, and the Community Shield.

Building Arsenal in Career Mode

  • Transfers - Sign new players and sell old players to continue to improve and adapt the squad.
  • Tactics - Edit the team's default tactics however you want! You can change the formation, player roles, and even which players take set pieces.
  • Player Development - Choose whether to provide minutes to young players at a low financial cost or buy already established, older players for guaranteed results.
  • Competing for Trophies - Lead your Arsenal side to glory in up to 6 competitions per season, with the option to play each and every game along the way!
