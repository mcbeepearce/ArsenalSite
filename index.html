About Arsenal FC

Arsenal Football Club is a professional soccer club based in North London, England. Having been founded in South London in 1886, the club eventually moved to North London, where they have since become one of the most prominent and recognizable teams in world soccer.

Arsenal's men's and women's teams currently play at the Emirates Stadium, which opened in 2006. When playing at the Emirates Stadium, they often wear their renowned red and white uniforms, or a variation of them. The modern team is known for its defensive resilience, commanding ball control, and aggressive pressure against opponents.

Important Facts

  • Arsenal has won the Premier League twice, once in an undefeated season in 2002/2003 and once in 2025/2026.
  • Arsenal holds the record for the most FA cups, having won the competition 14 times.
  • Martin Odegaard is the current club captain.
  • Arsenal boasted the best defense in world football during the 2025/26 season, conceding just 0.69 goals per game (41 goals in 59 matches).

Arsenal's Recent Success

  1. Arsenal won the Premier League in the 2025/2026 season, after three successive second place finishes.
  2. Arsenal finished as runner-up in the UEFA Champions League in the 2025/2026 season, missing out on penalties in the final.
  3. The club has won the Community Shield twice in the last four years.

Why Arsenal?

Arsenal has been a community-centered, yet globally-recognizable team and brand for over 100 years. The club's history and recent "comeback" under Mikel Arteta make it one of the most intriguing clubs in all of world football.
